# How to Reserve a Parking Space

You can reserve a space directly through this chatbot. The assistant will guide
you step by step and collect the details it needs.

## Information we collect

To make a reservation, we ask for:

1. **First name**
2. **Last name (surname)**
3. **Car number (license plate)**
4. **Reservation period** — the start date/time and end date/time you need the
   space for.

## Steps

1. Tell the assistant you want to book or reserve a parking space.
2. Provide your first name and surname.
3. Provide your car's license plate number.
4. Provide the start and end of your reservation period.
5. The assistant confirms the details and creates a **pending** reservation.
6. A human administrator reviews and confirms the reservation (this confirmation
   step is handled by staff).

## Notes

- A reservation holds a space of the requested type, subject to availability.
- You can cancel or change a reservation by contacting the information desk.
- Payment is completed on exit or through your monthly subscription.
- Your personal details are used only to manage your reservation and are handled
  according to our privacy policy.
