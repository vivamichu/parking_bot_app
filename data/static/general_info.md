# About Astana Central Parking

Astana Central Parking is a modern, secure multi-level parking facility located
in the heart of Astana. We provide safe, convenient, and affordable parking for
short-term visitors and long-term monthly subscribers.

## Overview

- 4 parking levels with a total of 420 spaces.
- 24/7 CCTV surveillance and on-site security staff.
- Well-lit, climate-sheltered indoor parking.
- Elevator and stair access to street level on every floor.
- Electric vehicle (EV) charging stations on Level 1.
- Dedicated spaces for people with disabilities near every elevator.

## Services

- Hourly, daily, and monthly parking options.
- Online and on-site reservation of parking spaces.
- Contactless entry and exit via license-plate recognition.
- Car wash service available on Level -1 (extra charge).

## Contact

For general inquiries, visitors can use this chatbot, the intercom at the
