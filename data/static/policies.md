# Policies

## Payment

- Accepted: contactless card, Kaspi QR, and cash at the pay stations.
- Hourly parking is billed per started hour.
- Daily rate applies automatically once cheaper than the accumulated hourly rate.
- Lost ticket / unreadable plate: a maximum daily rate is charged.

## Cancellation

- Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 1 hour before the start time.
- No-shows may be charged the first hour.

## Rules

- Park within the marked lines only.
- Do not leave valuables visible in your vehicle.
- The speed limit inside the facility is 10 km/h.
- Overnight stays are permitted for daily and monthly customers.

## Privacy

- Personal data collected for reservations (name, surname, license plate,
  reservation period) is stored securely and used only to manage your booking.
- We never share your personal data with third parties for marketing.
- You may request deletion of your reservation data at the information desk.
