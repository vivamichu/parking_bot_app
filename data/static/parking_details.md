# Parking Details

## Levels and space types

- **Level 1:** Standard spaces and EV charging stations. Closest to the exit.
- **Level 2:** Standard spaces and compact spaces.
- **Level 3:** Standard spaces and reserved monthly-subscriber spaces.
- **Level 4:** Standard spaces (usually the most available).
- **Level -1 (basement):** Premium wide spaces and the car wash bay.

## Space types

- **Standard:** Fits most sedans, hatchbacks, and small SUVs.
- **Compact:** Smaller footprint for small cars, at a lower rate.
- **Premium (wide):** Extra-wide spaces for large SUVs and vans.
- **EV:** Standard-sized spaces with a charging connector (Type 2 / CCS).
- **Accessible:** Wider spaces reserved for disability permit holders, near
  every elevator, free of the accessibility surcharge.

## Vehicle limits

- Maximum vehicle height: 2.1 m (Levels 1–4), 2.4 m (Level -1 premium).
- Motorcycles park free in the designated area near the Level 1 entrance.

## Security & safety

- 24/7 CCTV and roaming security staff.
- Emergency call points on every level.
- Fire suppression system throughout the facility.
