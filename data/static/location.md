# Location & Directions

Astana Central Parking is located at:

**12 Mangilik El Avenue, Yesil District, Astana, Kazakhstan.**

## How to find us

- Directly across from the Central Business District, next to the Nurly Zhol
  shopping center.
- The vehicle entrance is on Mangilik El Avenue; the exit is on Dostyk Street.
- GPS coordinates: 51.0900° N, 71.4180° E.

## Getting here

- **By car:** Take Mangilik El Avenue toward the Central Business District. The
  entrance ramp is signposted "P — Central Parking".
- **Public transport:** Bus routes 10, 12, and 40 stop at "Nurly Zhol", a
  2-minute walk from the entrance.
- **Accessibility:** Step-free access from the street to all levels via elevators.

## Nearby landmarks

- Nurly Zhol Shopping Center (adjacent)
- Central Business District towers (across the avenue)
- Yesil River embankment (600 m)
